### 📋 Overview & Purpose

I serve as an **Upazila Family Planning Assistant (UFPA)**, dedicated to maternal and child healthcare support, reproductive health advocacy, and community-level field administration. 

This repository functions as an open digital workspace to organize, track, and maintain:
- 📊 Field survey logs, reports, and beneficiary tracking systems
- 📑 Public health documentation, guidelines, and departmental records
- 🔬 Applied community health research papers, briefs, and demographic notes
- 📂 Standard operating procedures (SOPs) and informational toolkits

---

### 🏥 Core Competencies & Focus Areas

- **Family Planning Services:** Community outreach, contraceptive counseling, and resource distribution tracking
- **Maternal & Child Health (MCH):** Antenatal and postnatal record keeping, vaccination tracking, and nutrition education
- **Data & Documentation:** Field reporting, registry digitization, demographic monitoring, and statistical compilation
- **Research Archive:** Documenting grassroot health trends, healthcare delivery outcomes, and rural welfare initiatives
